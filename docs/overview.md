# Overview

Lamp gives one business a public status page and an operator workspace for
components, incidents, scheduled maintenance and subscriber communication.
Operators or accepted monitoring alerts provide component states; Lamp does not
probe services or measure uptime itself. An alert creates a draft incident for
an operator to review and publish.

## Request limits

Subscription requests are limited to 5 per minute from one requester network
address. Separately, an email address receives at most one confirmation message
in 24 hours, even if the subscription form is submitted again.
